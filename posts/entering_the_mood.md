# About how to enter in productive mood

The fist things that comes to my mind on this subject are the following. First, you must make sure you have slept enough, not too much and not to little but just enough. This sounds obvious but it's way harder than it might have look to me at first glance. 

I know for several years now that good sleep helps me find "the mood" where I can deliver 10 times what I can usually produce in the same amount of time. But still, having a strong and stable sleep routine is so hard for me.

Then, there is food. Ah... food, there is so much to say about it. I'm one of a kind that just find any pretext to eat something. Since many years now I notice the dead negative impact of sugar on my mood. This thing is just terrible, you eat something suggary and it's over, you can't focus for the next our and at the same time, sometime you just feel you need it. It's a drug for sure. 

Music can help enter in the mood, but also if you keep it for too long it tires you much quicker. At some point you head just feel heavy and you can't hear it anymore. Also, I need song very repetive without deep meaning in the lyrics, else my mind just get attract by it and I start loosing my focus. Electronic music is usually perfect helping me to enter the mood quickly. 

Also, something I recently discover, work routines. Work routines are any pattern you do over and over again every days, like ritual. They put your brain on confortable path, making you feel achieve something, before going into the unknow of fixing problems and bugs.
