# To be soothed while programming

About the importance of being in the correct mood to program.

> Understand why you need to program something, what is the greater purpose that motivate you to produce something.

Why this is important: Because if you want to produce something that will last long you need to understand what are the deep motivation of you action. This way you are sure to know that you are doing this for nothing.

I felt the need to code a markdown parser because of several things I noticed over the past few years:

## about myself

It's difficult to understand the world we live in, or at least I felt it's something really difficult. So, I feel the need to explain first to myself then to other what is my conception of the world. Then arise a question, on what support should I communicate those views on our matrix? Turns out not to be very exciting but markdown feels the write tool for this. It's supper simple to learn and once you wrote something it's easy to read. And guess what? It was design precisely for this purpose. Also, I felt way to overwhelm by all those note taking app, most of them are great but as soon as you change your device, or there is a new update everything suddently break and you feel terrible and demotivated again. I'm use to my coding set up (vim + what ever unix os). With this in hands I'm confident, I'll feel conftorbale writing something. The whole purpose of choosing raw markdown is both for it's simplicity as a writter to write something and for the reader to read it even it's not in nice mardown reader. This feeling is super motivating. It's like having supper power.

## why writting my own markdown parser

Again, we are back to why we do things. As I'm choosing this technology communicate to the world, there no other choice for me but to understand it from the inside. I need to learn what are the complete rules of it. Because understanding this will allow me to develop a critic look on it. See the quirks and beauty of it. By doing this I'll also deepen my learning on a programming language (Go in this case). It's nothing to say that understanding a new programming language requires deep practice of it. So, that's another good reason to follow the path of writing my own mardown parser.

Another reason why I want to complete this project from start to finish is that it will strengthen the emotional relationship of multiple fields I'm used to work with. To mention a few: writing english which is not my native language, improve my engineer skill, increase my ability to express my ideas clearly and re-inforce my confidence on sharing those ideas to the world. All of this, to me, are very important abilities an human should develop, and it feels even more important in this emerging AI era.

It's true that I something feel hopeless about what those algorithm are capable to produce, but the truth is that are not going to replace the immense joy there is to accomplish something by yourself.

All of those are reasons why I need to write my own markdown parser.
